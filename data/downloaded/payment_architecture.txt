Payment Service Architecture

The payment processing platform uses a microservices
architecture.

The Payment API receives incoming payment requests
and performs initial request validation.

Validated requests are published to an event streaming
platform for asynchronous processing.

The payment service uses idempotency keys to prevent
duplicate payment operations.

Transient failures are handled using exponential
backoff with jitter.

Failed events that exceed their retry policy are routed
to a dead-letter queue for investigation.

The system exposes health checks and distributed
tracing information for operational monitoring.
